## 🐸 About Me

I am a **biologist, scientific researcher, environmental manager, indie maker, and passionate programmer**. I work extensively with wildlife surveys, natural history, and animal taxonomy. I use computational biology, data science, and modern web technologies to turn complex field data into statistical models, publication-grade analytics, and offline-first digital tools for field teams.

- 🌿 **Wildlife surveying & biodiversity monitoring** in major infrastructure and conservation projects
- 🔬 **Taxonomy, Systematics & Evolution** (core focus on Anuran Amphibians and Reptiles; also medium/large mammals and birds)
- 📈 **Community ecology**: species richness, abundance, diversity indices (Shannon, Simpson), similarity (Jaccard, Bray-Curtis), and rarefaction curves
- 🗺️ **Biogeographical modeling & global biodiversity databases** (GBIF data mining with R `rgbif`, eBird datasets)
- 💻 **Environmental automation & field tools**: Progressive Web Apps (PWAs) engineered to work 100% offline in remote field conditions

## 🐍 Featured Project: Caderneta de Campo (Field Notebook PWA)

<a href="https://github.com/lucasaosf/caderneta-de-campo"><img alt="Caderneta de Campo — Field Wildlife Survey App" src="https://github.com/lucasaosf/caderneta-de-campo/raw/main/docs/banner.png" width="100%"></a>

Complete mobile Progressive Web App (PWA) for wildlife and botanical surveys in field operations: built-in taxonomic database with **4,903 Brazilian species**, vernacular names linked to scientific nomenclature, visual and auditory count logging, GPS waypoint tracking, and **automated technical Excel report generation** (species richness, diversity, rarefaction, similarity, Point Abundance Index). Operates **100% offline** on both Android and iPhone.

**[🚀 Launch Web App](https://lucasaosf.github.io/caderneta-de-campo/)** · **[🎥 Video Tutorial](https://lucasaosf.github.io/caderneta-de-campo/como-usar.html)** · **[📂 Source Code](https://github.com/lucasaosf/caderneta-de-campo)**

## 🪵 Featured Project: Cubagem Florestal (Smalian Forest Scaling)

<a href="https://github.com/lucasaosf/cubagem-florestal"><img alt="Cubagem Florestal — Smalian Log Scaling App" src="https://github.com/lucasaosf/cubagem-florestal/raw/main/docs/banner.png" width="100%"></a>

High-precision forestry dendrometry and log scaling app for field engineers: calculates volumetric timber yield for logging trucks (loaded/unloaded stacking factors), wood piles, and individual logs using the rigorous **Smalian Formula**. Features a native and exotic Brazilian dendrological catalog, UTM GPS coordinates, and **instant standardized Excel spreadsheet (.xlsx) exports**. Operates **100% offline** on Android and iPhone.

**[🚀 Launch Web App](https://lucasaosf.github.io/cubagem-florestal/)** · **[📂 Source Code](https://github.com/lucasaosf/cubagem-florestal)**

## 🐸 Sobre mim

Sou biólogo, pesquisador, gestor ambiental, indie maker e programador entusiasta. Trabalho com levantamentos de fauna, história natural e taxonomia, e uso programação para transformar dados de campo em análises, gráficos, relatórios e para criar ferramentas que facilitam o trabalho de campo.

- 🌿 Levantamento e monitoramento de fauna
- 🔬 Taxonomia, Sistemática e Evolução (com foco em Anfíbios Anuros e Répteis)
- 📈 Ecologia de comunidades: riqueza, abundância, diversidade, similaridade e rarefação
- 🗺️ Distribuição de espécies e bases de biodiversidade (GBIF, eBird)
- 💻 Automação de relatórios e ferramentas para o campo (PWAs)
